## 44. Research Contribution

### Contribution Statement

"The project contributes an integrated prototype that combines demand forecasting, inventory and warehouse analysis, explainable recommendations, and retrieval-augmented access to official government knowledge within a unified decision-support workflow for the Indian public food supply-chain context."

### Key Contributions

1. **Integration:** Combining established techniques (forecasting, rules, explainability, RAG) into one workflow
2. **Domain Adaptation:** Applying ML techniques to Indian public-sector supply-chain context
3. **Explainability:** SHAP explanations tailored to supply-chain decision factors
4. **Government-Data Grounding:** RAG using official Indian government documents
5. **Human-in-the-Loop:** Framework where recommendations support rather than replace human decisions

### What This Is NOT

- Novel ML algorithms
- Superior performance claims
- Production-grade system
- Official government deployment

### Value Proposition

- Student-implementable prototype
- Context-specific to Indian food supply chains
- Every prediction and recommendation is explainable
- End-to-end workflow from data to human decision
- Human-in-the-loop design suitable for government operations
