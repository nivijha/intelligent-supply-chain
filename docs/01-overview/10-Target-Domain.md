# Target Market / Domain

---

## 8. Target Market / Domain

### 8.1 Primary Domain

Indian public-sector food/agricultural supply chains, specifically:

- Food Corporation of India (FCI) operations
- State-level food distribution systems
- Public Distribution System (PDS) management
- Central and state warehousing operations

### 8.2 Target Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role in System | Primary Needs |
|-------------|---------------|---------------|
| **Government Planners** | Allocation and procurement decisions | Forecasts, recommendations, analytics |
| **Warehouse Managers** | Inventory and capacity management | Stock status, utilization metrics, alerts |
| **Supply-Chain Analysts** | Data analysis and reporting | Dashboards, charts, trend analysis |
| **Administrators** | System management and oversight | User management, system configuration |
| **Policy Personnel** | Policy compliance and review | RAG assistant for guidelines, audit logs |

### 8.3 Domain Context

The project addresses the Indian public food supply chain because:

- **Data availability:** Official government reports provide structured historical data
- **Policy documentation:** Government guidelines, circulars, and acts are publicly available
- **Social impact:** Effective supply-chain management directly affects food security
- **Decision complexity:** Multi-stakeholder, multi-region operations require analytical support
- **Transparency requirements:** Public-sector operations demand explainable decisions

### 8.4 Geographic Scope

The prototype focuses on:

- National-level data where available (FCI reports)
- State-level data for selected states
- Regional warehouse networks

The system is designed to be extensible to other states and commodities.

### 8.5 Clarification

This project is a **prototype decision-support platform** inspired by public-sector supply-chain challenges. It is **not officially used by any government organization**. The system demonstrates the feasibility of integrating ML, explainability, and RAG for supply-chain decision support.
