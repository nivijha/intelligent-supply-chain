# Why Human-in-the-Loop

---

## 31. Why Human-in-the-Loop

### Context

The system is a decision-support tool, not an autonomous decision-maker. Final operational decisions remain with authorized human users.

### Reasons

1. **Ground Reality:** Supply-chain decisions depend on factors not captured in data (weather, transportation, politics)
2. **Uncertainty:** ML predictions have uncertainty intervals that humans must consider
3. **Assumptions:** Rule-based recommendations have assumptions that may not hold in all situations
4. **Accountability:** Government supply chains require transparent, auditable decision trails
5. **Context:** Human operators have local knowledge no model can capture
6. **Ethics:** Regulatory requirements often mandate human oversight for resource allocation

### How It Works

1. System provides data, predictions, and recommendations
2. Human user reviews recommendations and explanations
3. User validates against ground knowledge
4. User accepts, rejects, or modifies recommendation
5. User takes operational action through existing channels
6. System logs decision for audit trail

### What the System Does NOT Do

- Automatically execute inventory movements
- Make autonomous allocation decisions
- Override human judgment
- Replace operational decision-makers

### Conclusion

The system supports human decision-making by providing data-driven insights, not by replacing human judgment.
